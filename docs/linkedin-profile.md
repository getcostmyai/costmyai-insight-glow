## 2. Overview / About (LinkedIn limit: 2,000 characters)

Paste this (1,631 characters):

```
Most teams have no idea what their AI is actually costing them, or how much of that bill is avoidable.

CostMyAI answers both. We show you what you overpay across the models and hosting providers you already use, we prove when a cheaper option is genuinely as good, and we can move your traffic across for you once it is proven.

What you get:

Compare. Live pricing and quality across the models and hosting providers you already use, with market data refreshed continuously. You see what the same work costs everywhere else, not just what you paid.

Certify. Before we call anything a saving, we check the cheaper model against a benchmark that still tells models apart today. If it does not hold up, we say so. A number without proof is not a saving.

Rightsize. Plenty of work runs on a model far bigger than it needs. We flag those cases and name the smaller model that does the same job.

Govern. Approve it once and switches happen on their own, re-checked against live benchmarks at the moment they run, never on a stale result from last month.

Why you can trust the number: no provider owns us, and no provider can pay for placement, ranking or routing preference. We have nothing to sell you except the truth of the number. If a benchmark stops separating models, we refuse to certify a saving on it rather than let a blind number pass as proof.

Getting started: Compare is free, and connecting takes a change to one base URL, not a rewrite. The paid rungs are confirmed with us directly during launch, no card needed to look.

CostMyAI is the neutral Financial Governance platform for AI spend.

See it at costmyai.com
```
